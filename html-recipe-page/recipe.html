Veg Fried rice

Now let's learn the process of veg fried rice

Ingredients

  • Cooked Rice
  • Oil or Butter
  • Prefered vegetables(carrot,beans,cabbage etc..)
  • chilli powder,salt,chopped garlic
  • soya sauce and pepper powder

Instructions

  1. Heat oil or butter in a wide pan
  2. Add the finely chopped garlic and spring onions and stir for 2 mins
  3. Now add all the veggies and toss them on high false for 5 mins
  4. Add salt and chill powder
  5. Now add the cooked rice and one spoon of light soya sauce
  6. Toss the rice on hih-flame for 3 mins
  7. Now pepper and vinegar.Toss the rice for 2 mins.

That's it your Fried Rice is readyy!! Serve it hot.
